How soon do you reply?

We respond to inquiries within 1 business day. Production lead time is quoted per model and volume.

When do I need 8-electrode vs 4?

Four foot electrodes are enough for a bathroom weight-and-estimate scale. Eight (handle + platform) is for segmental fat and muscle — the flagship body-composition line.
