Privacy

How this site handles the information you send us.

What we collect

The standard quote form requires name, email, inquiry type, product interest and annual volume. The advertising page requires name, company, email and request type. Other fields are optional. Inquiries may include campaign source fields, page paths, a submission time and an inquiry ID. We use these to reply and assess our B2B campaigns.

Who processes it

Submissions are emailed to hanhan@lefu.cc via FormSubmit. If that service is unavailable, the form opens your email client instead. We do not sell inquiry data.

How long we keep it

Inquiry records are kept as long as needed to support a sales conversation, then removed from active inboxes under our ordinary mail retention.

Your rights

Email hanhan@lefu.cc to access, correct, or delete an inquiry you sent. Device data from Unique Health products is governed by the app privacy notice, not this page.

Advertising measurement

LinkedIn advertising tracking is off until you accept. Your choice (version and expiry) is saved for 180 days. When accepted, LinkedIn may receive page visits and successful inquiry events, and campaign source is retained for this browser session. Without acceptance, no advertising source is stored; source parameters explicitly on the form page may still accompany that inquiry. Rejecting does not affect the form. Use the Privacy settings button on the Privacy page to change your choice. Withdrawal clears our saved campaign source and stops future tracking after reload; data already sent cannot be recalled. The LinkedIn partner ID is 9831228. We do not send inquiry field values to LinkedIn.
